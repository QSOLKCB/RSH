WHAT IS BEING COMPARED?

Quaternion paths, not screenshots.

Dzhanibekov reference

A rigid body with fixed principal moments I₁ < I₂ < I₃, initialized near the unstable intermediate-axis rotation. Its torque-free Euler equations conserve both |L| and rotational energy.

Jitterbug analogue

A positive-definite inertia tensor changes with a bounded periodic shape coordinate λ(t). It is a reduced variable-inertia model—not a multibody Fuller Jitterbug with vertices, hinges, constraints and internal momentum coupling.

Distance

d(q₁,q₂) = 2 acos(|q₁·q₂|) respects the fact that q and −q describe the same attitude. Global orientation, time shift and one scalar time rescaling are allowed during comparison.

Interpretation

“Partial resemblance” means both trajectories contain a large flip-like motif, but their complete attitude histories remain far apart. It is evidence for a research question, not identity of mechanism.
