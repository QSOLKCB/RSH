# Scientific boundary

RSH verifies software constructions.

For the geometry contract, it establishes that, for a stated configuration:

- prescribed curvature remains below its declared bound;
- prescribed torsion remains inside its declared open interval;
- the integrated frame remains numerically orthonormal within tolerance;
- an exact discrete midpoint exists and is translated to the origin;
- sampling is uniform in the path parameter;
- the encoded report is replay-identical under SHA-256;
- independent concurrent runs produce the same receipt.

For the constitutional tissue contract, it establishes that:

- cells are seeded from a verified geometry report;
- cell curvature and torsion remain inside the declared bounds;
- the shared tissue centroid is translated to the comparison origin;
- each tick extends an unbroken domain-separated receipt chain;
- declared sidecars remain under their residual gate or activate fallback;
- Q_f is calculated from the published functional factors;
- a dry-run refinement recommendation follows the declared objective order and
  human-ack rules.

It does **not** by itself establish a new law of nature, a quantum mechanism, a
physical substrate, an experimental measurement, a biological organism,
consciousness, sentience, subjective awareness, qualia, or autonomous agency.

A geometry receipt proves identity of encoded geometry evidence under its
runtime contract. A tissue receipt proves identity of one encoded simulation
history. Q_f compares functional cohesion factors inside that simulation.
None proves the truth of an external interpretation.

Any mapping from RSH samples or tissue metrics to reversible registers, audio,
rendering, hardware execution, cognition, biology, or social organization must
be described as a mapping unless separately validated by appropriate empirical
work.
